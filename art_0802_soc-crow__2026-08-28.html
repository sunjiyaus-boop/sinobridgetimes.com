다친 새끼 야생동물이 안쓰러워 집에 데려와 키운 40대가 법원에서 벌금형의 선고유예를 받았다. 야생생물법 위반이지만, 딱한 사정이 참작됐다.
양념자 기자 · 2026.08.28

보도에 따르면, 이 사람은 어미를 잃은 것으로 보이는 어린 야생 까마귀를 발견하고 집으로 데려와 돌봤다. 야생동물을 임의로 포획·사육하는 것은 관련 법에 저촉되지만, 법원은 동기와 정황을 고려해 선고유예 판단을 내렸다.

다친 야생동물을 발견하면 직접 데려오기보다 지역 야생동물 구조센터나 관할 기관에 연락하는 것이 안전하다. 전문가의 돌봄을 거쳐 자연으로 돌려보내는 것이 동물에게도 더 나은 길이기 때문이다.

野生動物保護規定，定人不得擅自捕捉或飼養野生動物。縱為善意，錯誤之方式亦可能危及動物之健康與生態系，發現時宜向專門機構通報。

若發現受傷之野生動物，較之親自攜回，聯繫地區野生動物救助中心或主管機關較為安全。經專家照料後送返自然，對動物亦為更佳之途。

'I Felt Sorry for the Chick': Person in Their 40s Who Raised a Wild Crow Gets a Suspended Fine

A person in their 40s who took in and raised an injured wild animal out of pity received a suspended sentence for a fine in court. It was a violation of wildlife law, but the sympathetic circumstances were taken into account.
By Yang Nyeom-ja · 2026.08.28

According to reports, the person found a young wild crow that appeared to have lost its mother and took it home to care for it. While capturing and keeping wild animals at will breaches the relevant law, the court issued a suspended sentence in light of the motive and circumstances.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Wildlife protection rules bar individuals from capturing or raising wild animals on their own. Even with good intentions, the wrong approach can harm an animal's health and the ecosystem, so reporting a find to a specialized agency is advisable.

If you find an injured wild animal, it is safer to contact a local wildlife rescue center or the responsible agency than to take it in yourself. Returning it to nature after expert care is the better path for the animal, too.
