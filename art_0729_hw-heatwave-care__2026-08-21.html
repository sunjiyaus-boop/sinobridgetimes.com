이런 점을 살펴요

Urging Safety for Chinese Merchants and Elders in the Heat: 'A Summer of Asking After One Another'

As record heat continues, everyday safety tips for Chinese-community merchants and elders are again in focus. The longer the heat lasts, the more it matters to check in on one another.
By Jeon Hong-bal · 2026.08.21

Chinese commercial districts such as Chinatown and markets often face the heat through outdoor activity and cooking. When heat alerts persist, both merchants and customers need to look after their health.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Points to watch

Following health authorities' advice, the basics are avoiding strenuous outdoor work during the hottest daytime hours and drinking water often. Shops can set up shaded, air-conditioned resting spaces, and it helps to frequently check on elders, the chronically ill and neighbors who work outdoors.

For elders living alone, a single call to ask after them or a bottle of water can be a great help. Cooling shelters and home health-check services run by local governments can also be actively used.

The community's summer

The heat is hard on everyone, but its weight grows lighter within a community that looks after one another. The mutual-aid culture of Chinese commercial districts shines all the more in a summer like this.

The Sinophone Bridge Times faithfully conveys safety information and cheers on the community's wisdom in crossing a difficult season together.
