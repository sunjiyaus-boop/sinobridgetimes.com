난터우현이 지역 곳곳에서 일대일 무료 영양 상담을 제공한다고 자유시보가 전했다. 만성질환 예방과 건강한 식습관 형성을 돕는 것이 목적이다.

전문가들은 예방 중심의 건강 관리가 장기적으로 의료비 절감에 기여한다고 본다. 지속 가능한 운영과 홍보가 과제로 꼽힌다.

華橋時報는 지역 건강 서비스 소식을 사실 중심으로 전하며, 예방 건강 정보를 함께 제공한다.

Nantou, Taiwan Rolls Out 'One-on-One Free Nutrition Counseling' Near Home; Expanding Local Health Services

Nantou County in Taiwan is advancing a service letting residents get one-on-one free nutrition counseling close to where they live—presented as a highly accessible local health-management model.
By Jeon Geum-mun · 2026.07.27

Nantou offers one-on-one free nutrition counseling across the county, the Liberty Times reported, aiming to prevent chronic disease and build healthy eating habits.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Nutrition counseling reviews an individual's health and diet to offer tailored menu and lifestyle advice, helping manage chronic conditions such as diabetes and hypertension.

Community-based services raise access for the elderly and vulnerable who find hospital visits hard, with links to local health centers and dietitians shaping results.

Experts say prevention-centered health management contributes to lower medical costs over the long term, citing sustainable operation and outreach as tasks.
