Global News Users Uneasy Over Political and Economic Turbulence; Restoring Trust Is the Task

A survey found news users worldwide growing uneasy amid successive political, economic and technological upheavals. The reshaping of long-standing international alliances and strain in the trade system were cited as the backdrop.
By Yeo Nyeon-jae · 2026.09.03

Per a media research institute's report, users worldwide are reacting to recurring political, economic and technological turbulence with fatigue and unease. A tendency to avoid news and declining trust were also observed.

Changes in the information environment, the influence of platforms and algorithms, and disinformation act in combination on trust in news. Accuracy and transparency are seen as keys to restoring trust.

Users' unease and avoidance bear directly on the health of the public sphere. The importance of providing accurate information and balanced reporting is again stressed.
