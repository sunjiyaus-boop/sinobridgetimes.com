제조업 원가

전력 다소비 업종인 화학·소재·금속 부문은 요금 인상이 곧바로 원가로 이어진다. 일부 기업은 자가발전 설비 확충과 야간 조업 확대를 검토하는 것으로 알려졌다.

가계 부문에서는 냉난방 비용 비중이 커지면서 소비 여력이 줄어든다는 지적이 나온다. 지역별 요금 격차도 논의 대상이다.

역내 파급

일본의 에너지 조달 조건 변화는 아시아 액화천연가스 현물 시장에도 영향을 준다. 한국과 대만도 같은 시장에서 물량을 확보하기 때문이다.

製造業成本

化學、材料與金屬等耗電產業，費率調漲即直接轉為成本。據悉部分企業正檢討擴增自家發電設備與擴大夜間作業。

家計部門則因冷暖氣費用比重上升，消費餘力減少之情形受到關注。地區間費率差距亦為討論對象。

Japan Raises Gas and Electricity Charges Together

Simultaneous increases in city gas and electricity charges in Japan place a double burden on households and manufacturers, attributed to fuel-cost adjustment mechanisms coinciding with the end of subsidies.
By Lee Mun-chang · 2026.09.10

City gas and electricity charges have risen together in Japan, increasing costs for households and manufacturers, in what analysts attribute to fuel-linked tariff calculations coinciding with the expiry of support measures.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Japan's heavy reliance on imported liquefied natural gas means international prices and the exchange rate feed through to tariffs relatively quickly. A weak yen adds to the effect.

Manufacturing costs

In electricity-intensive chemicals, materials and metals, higher tariffs translate directly into costs. Some firms are said to be weighing more on-site generation and expanded night-shift operation.

For households, a larger share of spending going to heating and cooling is said to erode consumption. Regional differences in tariffs are also under discussion.

Regional spillover

Changes in Japan's procurement conditions affect the Asian spot market for liquefied natural gas, where Korea and Taiwan compete for the same cargoes.

Should countries compete to build stocks ahead of the winter demand season, price volatility could increase.
