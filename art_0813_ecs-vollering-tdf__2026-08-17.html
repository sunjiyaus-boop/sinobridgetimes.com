외신에 따르면, 폴레링은 험준한 산악 구간에서 앞서 나가며 종합 우승을 확정했다. 여자 사이클의 위상이 높아지는 흐름을 상징하는 결과다.

여자 투르 드 프랑스는 근래 중계와 후원이 늘며 저변이 넓어지고 있다. 정상급 선수들의 경쟁은 종목의 인기와 상업적 가치를 함께 끌어올린다.

다만 남녀 대회 간 상금·처우 격차, 훈련 인프라 등은 여전한 과제로 지적된다. 지속 성장을 위해서는 제도적 뒷받침이 필요하다는 목소리가 크다.

Vollering Wins Women’s Tour de France

The Netherlands’ Demi Vollering won the Women’s Tour de France, taking the top spot with a commanding margin of more than a minute over second place.
By Yu Seon-gyeong · 2026.08.17

Per foreign outlets, Vollering pulled ahead on rugged mountain stages to secure the overall title—a result symbolizing the rising stature of women’s cycling.

광고 문의 · 300×250

The Women’s Tour de France has broadened its base recently with more broadcasting and sponsorship. Competition among top riders lifts both the sport’s popularity and commercial value.

Yet gaps in prize money and treatment between men’s and women’s events, and training infrastructure, remain noted challenges. Many call for institutional support for sustained growth.

Achievement in sport inspires across borders. For youth and amateur enthusiasts in the Chinese community, such a challenge is good motivation too.

This paper reports the result while weighing the sport’s growth and the tasks that remain.
