그의 이력은 홍콩 영화 산업의 흥망과 겹친다. 1970~80년대 홍콩 영화가 아시아 전역으로 유통되던 시기, 조연과 악역을 맡는 배우층이 두터웠고 그 층이 산업의 완성도를 뒷받침했다.

말년까지 그는 드라마와 영화에 간헐적으로 출연했다. 고령의 배우가 현역으로 남아 있는 것은 산업에 세대 간 연결 고리를 남기는 일이기도 하다.

재한 화인 사회에서도 그의 출연작은 익숙하다. 1980~90년대 홍콩 영화는 한국과 대만, 동남아 화인 사회가 공유한 문화 경험이었고, 세대 간 대화의 소재로 남아 있다.

By Jeon Geum-mun · 2026.09.05

Lau Siu-ming, a veteran of Hong Kong screen and stage, has died at the age of 94. He is reported to have used a wheelchair in his final years because of ill health.

He began in dance, studying modern dance in Europe before returning to Hong Kong and broadening into acting. The physical expressiveness of a trained dancer was long regarded as the mark of his performances.

He is best remembered for a formidable villain in a Hong Kong fantasy film of the 1980s, a role long discussed in Chinese-language popular culture and repeatedly referenced in later works.

His career overlaps with the rise and decline of the Hong Kong film industry. When its films circulated across Asia in the 1970s and 1980s, a deep bench of character and villain actors underpinned the industry's craft.

He continued to appear in film and television intermittently into old age. Veteran actors remaining active leave a generational link within an industry.

His work is familiar in Sinophone communities in Korea as well. Hong Kong films of the 1980s and 1990s were a shared cultural experience across Korea, Taiwan and Southeast Asia, and remain a subject of conversation between generations.
