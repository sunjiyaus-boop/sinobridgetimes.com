월가 전략가들은 단기 조정 위험이 높아졌다며 몇 가지 근거를 제시했다. 밸류에이션 부담, 에너지 비용 상승, 지정학 변수의 장기화, 실적 전망의 하향 가능성이 함께 거론된다.

물가와 금리

유가 상승은 운송·화학·항공 등 여러 산업의 비용으로 즉시 전이된다. 이 경우 물가 지표 둔화 흐름이 멈추고, 금리 인하 기대가 후퇴할 수 있다.

다만 에너지 가격 상승분이 근원 물가에 얼마나 반영되는지는 시차가 있어, 한두 달 통계만으로 방향을 단정하기는 어렵다는 신중론도 있다.

Oil Above $100 Sends Wall Street Lower on Renewed Inflation Worry

All three main New York indices fell as crude passed $100 a barrel, with strategists citing a higher risk of a short-term correction amid renewed concern over inflation.
By Ma Yak-ran · 2026.09.13

All three main indices in New York fell as crude oil passed $100 a barrel, on concern that higher energy prices will feed back into inflation readings.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Wall Street strategists set out several grounds for a higher risk of a near-term pullback: stretched valuations, rising energy costs, a prolonged geopolitical overhang and the possibility of downgraded earnings forecasts.

Prices and rates

Higher crude passes immediately into costs across transport, chemicals and aviation. That can halt the moderation in inflation readings and push back expectations of rate cuts.

There is a lag, however, in how much of an energy increase reaches core inflation, and some caution that a month or two of data is not enough to fix the direction.

Asian markets

Korea, Taiwan and Japan, all net importers of crude, are especially sensitive. Inventory gains at refiners and chemical makers appear alongside fuel costs for airlines and shipping lines.

The next markers are inflation releases in the major economies and the outcome of producer-country meetings, either of which could alter the short-term price path.
