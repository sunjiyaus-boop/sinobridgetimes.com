인권최고대표사무소는 기술의 개발과 배치 사이에 인권 영향 평가를 두어야 한다는 입장을 밝혀 왔다. 배치 이후의 사후 규제만으로는 피해 회복이 어렵다는 논리다.

기업의 자율 규범만으로는 부족하다는 지적도 나왔다. 구속력 있는 기준과 독립적인 감사 체계가 필요하다는 것이다.

다른 한편에서는 규제가 기술 발전을 늦춘다는 반론이 이어지고 있다. 각국의 산업 정책과 인권 기준 사이의 간격은 아직 좁혀지지 않았다.

한국과 대만도 관련 법제 정비를 진행 중이다. 개인정보 처리와 자동화 결정의 설명 의무가 공통 쟁점으로 다뤄진다.

U.N. Human Rights Chief Warns on AI, Urging Urgent Controls

The U.N. High Commissioner for Human Rights has warned of risks that artificial intelligence poses to human rights and urged governments and companies to take urgent control measures, citing surveillance, discrimination and constraints on free expression.
By Yeo Nyeon-jae · 2026.09.22

The U.N. High Commissioner for Human Rights has warned of the risks artificial intelligence poses to human rights and urged governments and companies to adopt urgent control measures.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Concerns cited include large-scale surveillance, discriminatory outcomes and a chilling of free expression, with a growing number of cases in which automated decisions directly affect individual rights.

The human rights office has argued that human rights impact assessments should sit between development and deployment, on the reasoning that after-the-fact regulation alone cannot repair harm.

It was also argued that corporate self-regulation is insufficient and that binding standards and independent auditing are required.

On the other side, the counterargument that regulation slows technological progress continues. The gap between national industrial policy and human rights standards has yet to narrow.

Korea and Taiwan are both working on related legislation, with personal data processing and duties to explain automated decisions treated as common issues.

This paper plans to track actual cases of application rather than relay the for-and-against framing. Whether public agencies disclose their use of algorithms is a verifiable indicator.
