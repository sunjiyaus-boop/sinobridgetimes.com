Trading Company Job Notice Posted on Incheon Association Board

A trading company has posted a recruitment notice on the Incheon Overseas Chinese Association board, seeking staff who work in both Chinese and Korean — an example of how hiring circulates within the community.
By Lee Mun-chang · 2026.09.11

A trading company posted a recruitment notice on the noticeboard of the Incheon Overseas Chinese Association, seeking staff who work in both Chinese and Korean.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Association noticeboards have long served as local information channels for jobs, rentals and events, circulating information along routes distinct from formal recruitment sites.

Trade and logistics are among the fields in which Chinese residents of Korea have long worked, since both language ability and familiarity with commercial practice in the two countries are required.

Demand has lately been growing for roles connected to e-commerce, customs agency work and international logistics platforms.

Working conditions and application details are set out in the notice on the association board.
