화교 학교와 서예

서예는 화교 학교의 교육 과정에서 오래 유지돼 온 과목이다. 한자 학습과 함께 진행되는 경우가 많아, 문자 교육과 예술 교육의 성격을 함께 갖는다.

지역 단위 대회 참가는 학생들에게 학교 밖의 평가 기준을 경험하게 하는 기회이기도 하다.

지역 사회와의 접점

화교 학교가 지역 대회에 참가하는 것은 학교와 지역 사회 사이의 접점을 만드는 방식이다. 서예처럼 언어 장벽이 낮은 분야에서 특히 그렇다.

부산 지역에서는 화교 학교와 지역 학교의 교류 행사가 이어져 왔다.

새 학기와 함께

Busan Chinese Primary School Announces Calligraphy Festival Winners

Busan Overseas Chinese Primary School published the list of its students' awards at the sixth Busan student calligraphy festival — an example of how school calligraphy instruction feeds into local competition.
By Kang Pae-san · 2026.09.05

The school published, through its notices page, the awards its students received at the sixth Busan student calligraphy festival.

광고 문의 · 300×250

The list of winners and results by category are available in the original notice.

Calligraphy in Chinese-heritage schools

Calligraphy has long been kept in the curriculum at these schools. Taught alongside character study, it carries both a literacy and an artistic function.

Entering local competitions also gives students experience of assessment standards outside their own school.

Contact with the wider community

Participation in local competitions creates points of contact between the school and the surrounding community — particularly in fields like calligraphy where the language barrier is low.

Exchange events between Chinese-heritage and local schools have continued in the Busan area.

Alongside the new term

The school had earlier posted its notice for the start of the first semester of the 115th academic year and an announcement about an autumn trip. Early-term events are running in sequence.

Notices for parents are posted to the school's board as they arise.
