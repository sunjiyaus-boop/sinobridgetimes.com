Overseas Chinese schools teach in an environment that uses both Chinese and Korean, so experience running bilingual classes matters in practice alongside subject knowledge.

Overseas Chinese schools in South Korea have continued in Incheon, Seoul and Busan among other cities. Changes in student numbers and the supply of teachers are challenges common to them all.
