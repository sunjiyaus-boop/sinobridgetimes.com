Boy Lost at Kaohsiung Tourist Site Reunited With Family After Police Review Over 100 Cameras

A boy who became separated from his family at a tourist site in Kaohsiung was reunited with them after police reviewed footage from more than 100 surveillance cameras, tracing his route step by step to pinpoint his location.
By Yeo Nyeon-jae · 2026.08.26

Separations at tourist sites tend to cluster in the busiest hours. Even a brief break in a guardian's line of sight can open a wide gap if the child walks the other way.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Reviewing camera footage is labour-intensive, requiring images from many devices to be stitched together in time order to reconstruct a route.

In the early response to a missing child, fixing the point and time of the last sighting accurately is known to matter most, as it narrows the search.

Writing a guardian's contact details on clothing or belongings, and agreeing a meeting point in advance, are commonly recommended precautions.

In this case, the investment of staff time in footage review produced a result, though not every case has the same conditions.

Similar procedures operate at crowded facilities in Korea, where the response system immediately after a report is considered decisive.
