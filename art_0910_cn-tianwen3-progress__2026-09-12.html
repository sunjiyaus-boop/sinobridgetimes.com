수석 과학자는 착륙 지점 선정이 과학적 가치와 착륙 안전성을 동시에 만족해야 한다고 설명했다. 고대 물의 흔적이 남아 있을 가능성이 높은 지형이 후보로 검토된다.

채취한 시료를 지구 대기권 재진입과 회수 과정에서 오염 없이 보관하는 기술도 핵심 과제로 꼽힌다. 지구 생물에 의한 역오염 방지 절차도 국제 기준에 맞춰 설계된다.

국제 협력의 여지

화성 표본 분석은 여러 나라 연구기관이 나눠 맡는 방식이 일반적이다. 시료 일부를 국제 공동 연구에 개방할지 여부는 앞으로의 관심사다.

발사 시기는 지구와 화성의 상대 위치에 따라 제한되며, 창이 열리는 시점에 맞춰 준비 일정이 조정된다.

China Details Progress on Tianwen-3 Mars Sample Return

The latest progress on the Tianwen-3 mission, which aims to return samples from Mars, has been made public. Its chief scientist said the landing site and sample containment are decisive for the mission.
By Yang Nyeom-ja · 2026.09.12

The latest progress on Tianwen-3, the mission intended to return samples from Mars, was disclosed, with the mission's chief scientist setting out preparations and scientific goals in an interview.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Tianwen-3 aims to collect material from the Martian surface and bring it to Earth, a multi-stage mission requiring an orbiter, a lander, an ascent stage and a return module to work in sequence.

Landing site and containment

The chief scientist said the landing site must satisfy both scientific value and landing safety. Terrain likely to retain traces of ancient water is among the candidates under review.

Keeping the collected material uncontaminated through atmospheric re-entry and recovery is also a central problem. Procedures against back-contamination by terrestrial organisms are designed to international standards.

Room for cooperation

Analysis of Martian samples is usually shared among research institutions in several countries. Whether part of the material will be opened to joint international study is a point of interest.

Launch timing is constrained by the relative positions of Earth and Mars, and preparation schedules are set around the opening of that window.
