당국은 육아 보조와 보육 시설 확충을 중심으로 대응해 왔다. 다만 지원 확대가 출생아 수 증가로 이어지기까지는 상당한 시차가 있다는 것이 여러 나라의 경험이다.

이민과 체류 정책도 인구 논의의 한 축으로 다뤄진다. 산업계에서는 인력 부족을 이유로 외국인 노동자 관련 제도의 조정을 요구해 왔다.

통계는 매월 발표되며, 계절적 요인에 따라 월별 변동이 나타난다. 다만 32개월이라는 기간은 단기 변동으로 설명하기 어려운 수준이다.

Taiwan's Population Falls for a Thirty-Second Straight Month

Figures show Taiwan's population has now declined for thirty-two consecutive months, with births continuing to fall short of deaths and the rate of decline settling in.
By Jo Gye-hyeon · 2026.09.13

Taiwan's population has fallen for thirty-two consecutive months. Data released by the authorities show births continuing to run below deaths.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Fewer marriages, a rising age at first birth and the cost of housing and child-rearing are cited together as background. Most analysis treats it as a structural trend that no single factor explains.

Changes in the age structure affect labour supply and social insurance finances with a lag. A shrinking school-age population has already prompted discussion of consolidating some schools.

The authorities have responded chiefly through child-rearing allowances and expanded childcare provision, though experience elsewhere suggests a long interval between such support and any rise in births.

Immigration and residence policy form another strand of the debate, with industry citing labour shortages in calling for adjustments to rules on foreign workers.

The statistics are published monthly and fluctuate with seasonal factors, but a run of thirty-two months is difficult to explain as short-term variation.
