台灣學校衛生法修正案有進展　強化學生健康管理

台灣立法院之學校衛生法修正案有所進展。內容為強化學生健康管理與學校衛生體系，著重於預防與安全。
本報記者 錢洪發 · 2026.08.24

據台北時報，修正案朝整修校內健檢與衛生、安全標準之方向討論，意在強化成長期學生之健康權保障。

광고 문의 · 300×250

修正案據悉廣泛涵蓋傳染病因應、供餐與環境衛生、心理健康支援等。學校現場之落實能力與財源確保，被視為實效之關鍵。

於教育與衛生交會之處，需細緻之制度設計。衡平調和學生、家長與教職員之負擔與效益，為其課題。

含華僑學校在內之地區教育機構，學生健康管理亦為共同關切。安全之學習環境，為孩子學習與成長之根基。

本報不預斷立法過程，並陳修正之方向與落實之爭點。

Taiwan Advances School Health Act Amendments, Strengthening Student Health Care

Amendments to Taiwan’s School Health Act advanced in the Legislative Yuan. Aimed at strengthening student health management and school hygiene systems, they emphasize prevention and safety.
By Jeon Hong-bal · 2026.08.24

Per the Taipei Times, the amendments were discussed toward revising in-school checkups and hygiene and safety standards, intending to bolster the health rights of growing students.

광고 문의 · 300×250

The amendments reportedly cover infectious-disease response, meal and environmental hygiene, and mental-health support broadly. Schools’ implementation capacity and funding are seen as keys to effectiveness.

Where education meets public health, careful institutional design is required. Balancing the burdens and benefits for students, parents, and staff is the task.

For local education institutions including overseas Chinese schools, student health management is a shared concern. A safe learning environment is the foundation of children’s study and growth.

This paper does not prejudge the legislative process; it presents the direction of the amendments and the issues of implementation.
