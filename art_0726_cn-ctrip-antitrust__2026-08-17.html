China's Largest Travel Platform Ctrip Fined Over Antitrust Violations

Ctrip, China's largest online travel platform, was heavily penalized for two monopolistic practices, with a fine equivalent to about ₩24.8 billion, Taiwanese media reported.
By Jo Gye-hyeon · 2026.08.17

Ctrip, China's largest travel platform, was heavily fined by regulators over two monopolistic practices, United Daily News reported, with the penalty put at about ₩24.8 billion.

Regulators have targeted platform operators' abuse of dominant market positions, and this action is read as an extension of that regulatory trend.

Online travel platforms form a vast brokerage market spanning airfares, lodging and local products; a dominant player's pricing and contract practices affect both consumers and listed vendors.

How the penalty changes the industry's commission structure and listing terms is being watched, with some calling it regulation that weighs consumer benefit and market competition together.

Ctrip's specific response was not confirmed. The industry notes the possibility of further platform regulation.
