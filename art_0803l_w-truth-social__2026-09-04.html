트럼프 미국 대통령이 관여한 소셜미디어 '트루스소셜'이 게시물을 월가 투자자에게 유료로 미리 제공하는 서비스를 추진하면서 논란이 커지고 있다. 정보 접근의 공정성 문제가 제기됐다.
형덕창 기자 · 2026.09.04

보도에 따르면 해당 서비스는 대통령의 게시물을 일반 공개보다 앞서 유료 구독자에게 제공하는 방식이다. 시장에 영향을 줄 수 있는 정보의 사전 제공이 형평성 논란을 낳고 있다.

Trump's 'Truth Social' Pushes Paid Early Access to Posts, Fueling Controversy

Truth Social, the social-media platform tied to President Trump, is pushing a service offering paid early access to posts for Wall Street investors, fueling controversy over the fairness of information access.
By Hyeong Deok-chang · 2026.09.04

Per reports, the service would provide the president's posts to paying subscribers ahead of general release. Advance provision of potentially market-moving information has raised fairness concerns.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Critics worry that delivering an official's remarks first to paying users could cause information asymmetry and market distortion. Others counter that it is a legitimate commercial service.

Because politicians' and officials' remarks can affect markets and policy, the fairness of how they are released is important, observers note. Regulators' judgment is also of interest.

Hwagyo Sibo reports this from the standpoint of information fairness rather than for or against any individual, presenting arguments on both sides.
