광고 문의 · 300×250

Taiwan Falls 0-2 to South Korea at the U18 Asian Baseball Championship

Taiwan lost 2-0 to South Korea at the U18 Asian Baseball Championship. In a match between two front-line left-handed starters, Taiwan's batting order failed to score, making the path to the title considerably harder.
By Im Suk-bun · 2026.09.07

Taiwan lost 2-0 to South Korea at the U18 Asian Baseball Championship, in what Taiwanese media described as a meeting of top left-handed starters.

광고 문의 · 300×250

The completeness of the two starting pitchers decided the game. Taiwan put runners on base several times but the decisive hit did not come.

South Korea has continued a finely divided approach to its pitching staff in recent international youth tournaments, changing pitchers while their stuff holds rather than extending starters deep into games.

The defeat leaves Taiwan poorly placed in the race for the title. Advancement will depend on remaining results and the combination of outcomes among other teams.

Youth tournaments are treated as the stage before senior national selection. A sizeable share of the players here have been named as draft prospects for professional clubs in their respective countries.

Taiwan and South Korea have met repeatedly in youth baseball over many years. Both draw on a broad base of high-school baseball as the foundation for results.
