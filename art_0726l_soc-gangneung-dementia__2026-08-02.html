광고 문의 · 300×250

치매 환자는 방향 감각과 판단력이 떨어져 실종 시 위험이 크다. 특히 폭염기에는 탈수·온열질환 위험이 겹친다.

실종 초기 신속한 수색과 위치 확인 체계가 생존율을 좌우한다. 배회감지기 등 예방 장치의 보급 필요성이 제기된다.

고령화가 진행되며 치매 노인 실종은 사회적 과제로 커지고 있다. 지역사회의 촘촘한 돌봄망이 요구된다.

Missing Dementia Patient in His 80s Found Dead Two Days Later in Gangneung

A missing elderly dementia patient was found dead after two days, underscoring the importance of response for vulnerable groups.
By Yeo Nyeon-jae · 2026.08.02

An elderly dementia patient in his 80s who went missing in Gangneung, Gangwon, was found dead two days later, the Kyunghyang Shinmun reported.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Dementia patients, with impaired orientation and judgment, face high risk when missing—compounded by dehydration and heat illness in summer.

Fast early searches and location systems shape survival; the case raises the need to spread preventive devices like wandering detectors.

As aging advances, missing dementia patients grow into a social challenge, demanding a fine-meshed community care net.

We report the safety of vulnerable groups on the facts.
