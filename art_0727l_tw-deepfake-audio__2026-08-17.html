By Lee Mun-chang · 2026.08.17

As an AI-faked presidential audio spread, the ruling and opposition camps clashed, the Liberty Times reported, with one side defending it and the other calling it 'unlawful.'

광고 문의 · 300×250

Deepfakes use AI to finely mimic real people's voices and faces and carry high risk of misuse for disinformation, fraud and defamation—especially sensitive in electoral and political settings.

Experts say labeling duties, detection technology and swift blocking of distribution must go together, with the balance of free expression and regulation also at issue.

Authorities are reviewing legality and sought platform operators' cooperation, while stressing media-literacy education for voters.
