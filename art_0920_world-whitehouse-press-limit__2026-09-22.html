美國法院向來將政府以報導內容為由差別限制特定記者或媒體接觸一事，視為憲法上之問題。白宮採訪證遭撤銷之案件中，法院要求給予程序保障之判例即為代表。

惟白宮得以官邸空間與門禁管理之裁量權為依據，主張決定何人得進入有限空間屬營運判斷。爭點集中於該裁量是否與對報導內容之不滿相連結。

採訪接觸看似形式問題，實務上卻左右資訊之數量與速度。簡報現場之提問機會、背景說明之出席與否、隨行採訪團之登機資格，均與進入權限相繫。

此案於他國新聞環境亦具參考價值。政府機關門禁與記者團運作方式各國不一，惟對接觸權限可能與報導立場掛鉤之疑慮，則屬共通。

White House Restricts Access for CNN and Two Other Outlets

The White House has moved to restrict building access for three news organizations including CNN, prompting a dispute over press freedom. Legal scholars say barring reporting access because of coverage may run afoul of the First Amendment.
By Yeo Nyeon-jae · 2026.09.22

The White House notified the outlets that their access privileges were being withdrawn. The organizations involved view the step as retaliation for their reporting, and the White House press corps has been discussing a collective response.

광고 문의 · 300×250

U.S. courts have long treated discriminatory restrictions on particular reporters or outlets based on content as a constitutional problem. A case in which a White House credential was revoked, and the court required procedural protections, is the leading precedent.

The White House can point to its discretion over the building and its access management, arguing that deciding who enters a confined space is an operational judgment. The issue turns on whether that discretion is tied to displeasure with coverage.

Access looks like a formality but in practice governs the volume and speed of information. Opportunities to ask questions at briefings, attendance at background sessions and eligibility to travel with the pool are all bundled with credentials.

The episode is a reference point for press environments elsewhere. Rules on entering government buildings and running press corps differ by country, but the concern that access can be linked to editorial line is shared.
