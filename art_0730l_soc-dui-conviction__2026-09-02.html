장입군 기자 · 2026.09.02

안전한 도로는 예외 없는 원칙에서 나온다. 화인·이주민을 포함한 모든 운전자가 음주운전 금지라는 기본을 지키는 것이 지역사회 전체의 안전으로 이어진다.

「事故後才喝酒」酒駕無罪遭翻　通聯紀錄致判1年

交通事故後為避酒測，主張「事故後才喝酒」而於一審獲無罪之四旬男子，因通聯紀錄露餡，終遭判有期徒刑1年。
本報記者 張立群 · 2026.09.02

據경향신문，主張事故後才喝酒而擺脫酒駕嫌疑之四旬駕駛，因通聯紀錄等情況證據使主張遭翻，判有期徒刑1年，係暴露「事後飲酒」主張破綻之案例。

광고 문의 · 300×250

酒駕後主張追加飲酒之所謂「事後飲酒」，係欲動搖測值可信度以逃避處罰之手法。惟多因通聯、移動紀錄等客觀證據遭駁。

法院綜合供述之一致性與情況證據判斷事實。數位紀錄細密呈現時間與行蹤，發揮篩除不實主張之作用。

酒駕係威脅自身與他人生命之重大犯罪。若再加以逃避處罰之企圖，罪質將被評為更重。

安全之道路源自無例外之原則。含華人、移民在內之所有駕駛，守住禁止酒駕之基本，即連結至全社區之安全。

'I Drank After the Crash': Drunk-Driving Acquittal Reversed to One Year Over Phone Records

A man in his 40s who claimed he 'drank after the crash' to avoid a breath test and was acquitted at first instance was ultimately sentenced to a year in prison, undone by his phone records.
By Jang Ip-gun · 2026.09.02

According to Kyunghyang Shinmun, a driver in his 40s who had escaped a drunk-driving charge by claiming he drank after the crash had his claim overturned by circumstantial evidence including phone records and was sentenced to one year — a case exposing the flaw in the 'after-the-fact drinking' defense.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Claiming additional drinking after drunk driving — 'after-the-fact drinking' — is a tactic to shake the reliability of a reading and dodge punishment, but it is often rebutted by objective evidence such as call and movement records.

Courts judge facts by weighing the consistency of statements and circumstantial evidence. Digital records show time and movement in fine detail, filtering out false claims.

Drunk driving is a serious crime that threatens one's own and others' lives, and an attempt to evade punishment on top of it is judged as more culpable.

Safe roads come from principles without exception. When every driver, Chinese and migrant residents included, keeps the basic ban on drunk driving, it leads to the whole community's safety.
