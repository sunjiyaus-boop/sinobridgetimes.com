미국 장기 국채 수익률도 함께 내렸다. 최근 19년 만의 최고 수준까지 올랐던 30년물 금리가 되밀린 형태다. 금리와 유가가 같은 방향으로 움직이면 물가 압력에 대한 시장의 경계가 완화됐다는 신호로 읽히곤 한다.

대만 야간 선물이 오른 배경에는 반도체 관련 종목에 대한 기대가 깔려 있다. 조달 비용 부담이 줄면 설비 투자 계획에 여유가 생긴다는 계산이다.

本報記者 錢洪發 · 2026.08.30

油價下跌解讀為供給端疑慮稍見緩和所致。惟中東航路情勢已生根本變化之依據尚屬闕如，故回檔之可能性仍屬敞開。

美國長期公債殖利率亦一同走低。近期曾升至十九年新高之三十年期利率呈回落之勢。利率與油價同向而動時，往往解讀為市場對物價壓力之戒心趨於緩和之訊號。

Oil Prices and US Treasury Yields Fall Together; Taiwan Index Night Futures Up Over 200 Points

International oil prices and US Treasury yields declined together, and Taiwan's weighted index night futures responded with a gain of more than 200 points.
By Jeon Hong-bal · 2026.08.30

The drop in crude is read as a modest easing of supply-side concern. There is as yet no evidence of a fundamental change in Middle East shipping conditions, so a reversal remains possible.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Long-dated US yields fell as well, pulling back the 30-year rate that had recently reached a 19-year high. When yields and oil move in the same direction, markets often read it as reduced wariness about price pressure.

Behind the rise in Taiwan's night session lies expectation around semiconductor names, on the calculation that lighter funding costs give capital-spending plans more room.

A single day's move is not enough to fix a direction. Over recent weeks oil and yields have swung widely on geopolitical headlines.

Regular-session reactions in Korea and Taiwan will be visible after the open. This paper offers confirmed figures rather than forecasts.
