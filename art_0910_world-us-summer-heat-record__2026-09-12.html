야외 노동자와 고령자, 냉방 접근이 제한된 저소득 가구가 가장 큰 영향을 받는다는 점은 여러 조사에서 확인돼 왔다.

研究人員將此一紀錄解讀為人為氣候變遷之最新訊號，並說明其幅度與持續期間難以僅以自然變動解釋。

社會影響

酷暑推升電力需求，夏季尖峰用電紀錄屢遭刷新。冷氣費用負擔與熱傷害發生亦一併增加。

戶外勞動者、高齡者與冷氣取得受限之低所得家戶所受影響最大，此點已於多項調查獲得確認。

韓半島、中國東部與日本近年亦接連出現夏季高溫紀錄。各地成因構成雖異，長期趨勢則指向同一方向。

Contiguous United States Records Its Hottest Summer

Average temperatures across the contiguous United States were the highest in the observational record this summer, which researchers read as the latest signal of human-driven climate change.
By Bang Mun-yeon · 2026.09.12

Average temperatures across the contiguous United States were the highest on record this summer, measured as a national average for June to August, with individual state records also broken.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Researchers described the result as the latest signal of anthropogenic climate change, saying the magnitude and duration are hard to explain by natural variability alone.

Social effects

The heat pushed electricity demand to repeated summer peaks. Cooling costs and heat-related illness rose with it.

Surveys have consistently found outdoor workers, older people and low-income households with limited access to cooling to be the most affected.

A comparison with East Asia

The Korean peninsula, eastern China and Japan have also seen a run of summer heat records. The mix of causes differs by region, but the long-run trend points the same way.

The next markers are winter records and sea-surface temperatures, both of which feed into forecasts for the following summer.
