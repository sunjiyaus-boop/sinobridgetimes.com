Formaldehyde, a harmful substance, was detected in traditional rice-noodle products in Taiwan's Hsinchu, sparking a food-safety controversy; the opposition faulted the local government's oversight and authorities began recalls and investigation.
By Yeo Nyeon-jae · 2026.08.11

Formaldehyde was found in Hsinchu-area rice-noodle (chuifen) products, the United Daily News and other Taiwanese media reported, with the DPP pointing to gaps in oversight.

광고 문의 · 300×250

Formaldehyde is banned for illegal use as a preservative or bleach; when found in food, recalls and cause investigations follow, and authorities are tracing distribution routes and raw materials.

Consumer anxiety and response

Local residents and consumer groups demanded a clear cause and prevention of recurrence; authorities signaled halting sales of the affected products and expanding testing.

Experts say raw-material management and hygiene at processing and storage stages are key, citing stronger regular inspections of small manufacturers as a task.
