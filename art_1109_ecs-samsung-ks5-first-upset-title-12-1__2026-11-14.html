승부를 가른 5회 8득점

경기는 5회에 사실상 끝났다. 중앙데일리에 따르면 삼성은 5회에만 8점을 뽑았고, 이 중 6점은 KT 유격수 권동진의 실책 2개가 겹치며 나온 비자책점이었다. 김지찬이 2타점 3루타를 쳤고, 코리아타임스는 최형우의 적시타와 이재현의 2루타도 이어졌다고 소개했다.

선발 원태인은 5이닝 1실점으로 승리투수가 됐다. KT 선발 데이비스 대니얼은 3회를 끝으로 마운드를 내려갔다. 이후 KT 구원 투수 기용을 두고는 두 매체의 설명이 달라 본지는 확인된 범위만 적는다.

MVP 구자욱과 우승 서사

한국시리즈 MVP는 주장 구자욱이 차지했다. 코리아타임스에 따르면 그는 왼쪽 햄스트링 부상을 안고도 5차전에서 5타수 3안타 2득점을 기록했고, 중앙데일리는 시리즈 타율이 5할이었다고 전했다. 박진만 감독은 사령탑으로서 첫 우승을 맛봤고, 41세 포수 강민호는 프로 22년 차에 처음 우승 반지를 끼게 됐다.

시리즈는 쉽지만은 않았다. 중앙데일리에 따르면 삼성은 수원 1차전에서 0-8로 끌려가다 7점을 따라붙고도 7-8로 졌으나, 2차전을 12회 접전 끝에 3-2로, 3차전을 5-1로, 4차전을 10-5로 잡았다. 3차전에서는 후라도가 7이닝 1실점으로 KT 타선을 눌렀다.

KT는 구단 최다인 정규시즌 90승으로 한국시리즈에 직행했지만 타선이 끝내 살아나지 못했다. 8회에는 유격수 권동진이 타구에 얼굴을 맞아 구급차로 이송됐으며 이후 상태는 보도에서 확인되지 않았다. 코리아타임스는 삼성이 2018년 SK 이후 2위 팀으로 우승한 첫 사례라고 소개했다.

Samsung beat KT 12-1 in Game 5 to win the Korean Series 4-1, ending a 12-year wait with an upset of the 90-win favorites

Samsung beat KT 12-1 in Game 5 of the 2026 KBO Korean Series at Daegu Samsung Lions Park on the 8th, closing the series 4-1. According to Korea JoongAng Daily it is the club’s first title since 2014 and its ninth overall, and the Chosun Ilbo called it the franchise’s first Korean Series upset over a regular-season top seed.
By Ma Yak-ran · 2026.11.14

The Korea Times reported a sellout crowd of 23,550 in Daegu. KT struck first on a Jang Jin-hyuk sacrifice fly in the third, but Samsung answered with three runs in the bottom half, and JoongAng Daily said Diaz’s single drove in the go-ahead run.

Eight runs in the fifth decided it

The game was effectively settled in the fifth. JoongAng Daily said Samsung scored eight times that inning, six of them unearned after two errors by KT shortstop Kwon Dong-jin. Kim Ji-chan hit a two-run triple, and the Korea Times added that Choi Hyoung-woo had an RBI single and Lee Jae-hyeon a double.

Starter Won Tae-in earned the win with five innings of one-run ball, while KT starter Davis Daniel was out after the third. The two outlets differ on which KT reliever followed, so this report sticks to what could be confirmed.

MVP Koo Ja-wook and the title story

Captain Koo Ja-wook was named series MVP. The Korea Times said he played through a left hamstring injury and went 3-for-5 with two runs in Game 5, while JoongAng Daily put his series average at .500. Manager Park Jin-man won his first title as skipper, and 41-year-old catcher Kang Min-ho got his first ring in his 22nd pro season.

It was not smooth throughout. JoongAng Daily said Samsung rallied for seven runs after trailing 8-0 in Game 1 in Suwon but lost 8-7, then won Game 2 3-2 in 12 innings, Game 3 5-1 and Game 4 10-5. In Game 3, Jurado held KT to one run over seven innings.

KT went straight to the Series with a club-record 90 regular-season wins but never got its lineup going. In the eighth, shortstop Kwon was struck in the face by a ground ball and taken away by ambulance; no update on his condition was available. The Korea Times noted that Samsung is the first No. 2 seed to win the title since the 2018 SK Wyverns.
